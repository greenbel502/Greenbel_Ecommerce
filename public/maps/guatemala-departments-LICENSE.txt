The downloaded source is provided without modification at:
/maps/guatemala-departments-source.geojson

The local derived map geometry at /maps/guatemala-departments.json is made
available under ODbL 1.0 as well. Changes: Web Mercator projection to a 600 × 660
SVG view, Douglas–Peucker simplification (0.35 SVG unit), rounded projected
coordinates, slug identifiers and representative interior department centers.
It contains only departmental geometry, not GreenBel/cooperative locations.
The company's configured locations are an independent database.

This visualization is for locating registered offices, not navigation or a
statement of international borders. When exact office coordinates are absent,
the marker is an approximate position within its department and is labeled as
such. Addresses are supplied and maintained by the administrator.
